My Story

I chose HR because I enjoy understanding people — what motivates them, what gets in their way, and how good systems can help them do their best work. For me, HR is not about control. It is about making work easier and more meaningful for everyone.

I learn quickly and enjoy working on problems that do not have a clear answer yet. I like to understand the real issue first, then build something that actually works — not just a short-term fix.

My goal is to grow as an HR Business Partner — someone who sits close to the business, understands what teams need, and helps the organization move forward through its people. This role at Joyful Infinity is the right place to start that journey.

Joyful Infinity thinks ahead about health — I want to bring the same mindset to HR here. Set up the right systems early, so people and the company can grow together.
